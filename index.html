Platform Architecture

Built for Financial Precision

Engineered to separate liquid spendable funds from allocated investment principal while keeping every calculation deterministic and auditable.

Dual-Wallet Custody Segregation

Liquid Exchange balances (for deposits and withdrawals) remain strictly segregated from locked OTC investment principal until contract maturity.

Deterministic Simple-Interest Math

Returns follow straightforward full-duration simple interest (`principal × (ROI / 100)`) with elapsed accrual derived from authoritative timestamps.

Immutable Purchase Snapshot

Each customer contract stores a permanent snapshot of agreed ROI, duration, start time, and maturity time—unaffected by future catalogue edits.

Near-Real-Time Portfolio Sync

Dashboards reflect active allocations immediately after confirmation, showing elapsed cycle days, accrued profit, and full transaction ledger entries.

Automated Maturity Settlement

Scheduled serverless settlement checks matured contracts, locks records against duplicate execution, and credits principal plus profit atomically.

Introducing Broker Program

Institutional and wealth partners gain dedicated referral attribution and multi-tier volume reporting through our partner portal.

Security & Auditability

Engineered for Ledger Integrity

Every balance movement, investment subscription, and maturity payout is enforced inside Neon PostgreSQL transactions with strict validation and duplicate protection.

ACID Database Transactions

Plan validation, wallet debits, investment record creation, and ledger writes commit together or roll back cleanly on any failure.

Idempotent Requests & Concurrency Locks

Unique transaction references and row-level database locks prevent double debits, overlapping cron runs, or duplicate settlement credits.

Fixed-Point Precision & Audit Trail

Financial values use exact PostgreSQL numeric columns alongside integer-cent settlement arithmetic to prevent floating-point rounding drift.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers covering plan mechanics, simple-interest calculations, wallet custody, and maturity settlement.

How are investment returns calculated on PrimeHavenCapital?
All investment plans use deterministic simple interest over the plan's full duration rather than compounding. Total profit is calculated as principal × (roi_percentage / 100). Both the ROI rate and projected total payout are shown transparently before you confirm an investment.
What is the difference between Short-Term and Long-Term plans?
Short-Term plans are designed for shorter investment durations with lower minimum commitments, while Long-Term plans carry extended contract durations and higher capital limits. Active plans in both categories are published directly from our PostgreSQL plan catalogue above.
Will my investment terms change if an administrator edits a plan later?
No. At the exact moment your investment is confirmed, the platform saves an immutable snapshot of your plan's roi_percentage, duration_days, start timestamp, and maturity timestamp on your contract record. Subsequent plan updates only affect new subscriptions.
How does automatic maturity settlement work?
Once an investment contract reaches or passes its maturity timestamp, scheduled settlement processing verifies eligibility under a database transaction lock, credits your principal and simple-interest profit back to your liquid Exchange Wallet, creates an auditable ledger record, and marks the contract as completed.
How do Exchange Wallet and OTC Investment balances work together?
Confirmed deposits credit your spendable Exchange Wallet. When you subscribe to a plan, the principal transfers into your active OTC allocation while staying visible in your dashboard. Once the contract settles at maturity, your funds return to your Exchange Wallet, where they can be reinvested or withdrawn.
How do I begin if I do not have an account yet?
Click Get Started to create an account and verify your email with the 6-digit code. Once signed in, fund your Exchange Wallet via the Deposit desk, then click Invest Now on any active Short-Term or Long-Term plan to allocate capital immediately.
